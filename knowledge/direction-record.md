## The final ruling: the demand test

The chat's last message (agent, 2026-08-24) stopped ruling on arguments entirely:

> reachable, paying ICP for ALTERED exist at your current audience level? It is testable in two

**The demand test, as proposed (originally ending Sept 8):**

- One truth artifact: an all-in-one article - pain, promise, mechanism, buy-in. 80% and shipped.
- One stable, honest conversion object: a reservation deposit with a physical care package.
  Deposit amount and the $12,500 anchor stay unpriced until five real ICP conversations happen.

- Pre-committed pass thresholds: 10 real replies + 5 genuine problem conversations + (3 booked
  calls or 1 unprompted "how do I pay").

- Fail: external proof the ICP is not reachable at current audience size; the pre-named inheritor
  takes over without new essays (SMS or the job floor).
- During the test, flips are logged as challenges but cannot move the ledger. Only the result can.

The owner never explicitly confirmed the test in that chat - it errored first - but his 2026-08-27

## Mechanisms discovered, worth keeping forever

These explain the owner's flip pattern and are product insight for ALTERED itself:

- **Two currencies.** Building ALTERED pays identity daily and starves time and money. Money-first
  paths pay time and money and starve identity. A flip happens when the starved currency screams.
  The fix is a scheduled stipend to the starved currency (the "dose"), not willpower.
- **Vividness asymmetry.** Flips happen when the committed path's costs turn vivid while the
  alternative's costs are still abstract. Forecastable: day 3-5 after any commitment.
- **The complexity tax.** Everything ALTERED-shaped needs distillation work (value proposition,
  messaging, models, plans) that only happens at high energy. Optimistic estimates run 3-10x low,
  by the owner's own record ("two days means two weeks").
- **Attraction is not adherence, and adherence is not optional arithmetic.** A plan's value is its
  conversion math times the probability the owner executes it. He returns to ALTERED in every
  state; he abandons off-lane paths in under 30 hours. Both facts are measured, not vibes.
- **Challenge protocol.** A path change must quote the specific recorded reason it overturns and
  defeat it. Failed challenges get logged and counted. This was run manually in the chat and it
  worked - it converted a loop into an amendment. It is the conceptual seed of the decisions
  ledger as enforced data (and of ALTERED's own reasoning-retention product idea).
