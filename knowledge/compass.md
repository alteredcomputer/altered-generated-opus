## The problems we solve, in the buyer's language

From D019 and the direction record:

1. Starting down a path and never finishing or clarifying it - switching ships under pressure.
2. Un-deciding: re-deriving conclusions you already reached, because nothing holds them.
3. A thousand scattered thoughts and no source of truth, so the value proposition never gets
   clear enough to ship or to market consistently.
4. Context re-explained to every AI chat from zero, producing generic output.
5. Consistency: nothing reminds you, so the important quiet work drops.

We never promise revenue. The outcome chain: get crystal clear on what your product is,
consolidate the scattered thoughts into the few that matter, build product and marketing around
those, ship, hold steady.
