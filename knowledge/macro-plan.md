## Standing rules that protect the plan

- Between checkpoints, path challenges are logged, not acted on. A challenge must quote and defeat
  the recorded reason it overturns (direction record).
- The bet never carries the rent. The floor is decided by calendar, not by optimism. "We will
  attempt the latter with a fallback to the former" (source archive, 2026-09-04).
- No hand-written code before sales except the runway (D072, D080, Q60 pending). Anything
  ALTERED-core is his.
- Everything here is generated and disclosed; outbound writes stay default-deny with per-operation
  approval and kill switches.
