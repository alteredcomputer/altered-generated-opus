# Macro plan

Proposed 2026-08-27, pending the Round 5 answers registered in `open-questions.md`. Phases are
gated by evidence and dates, not by mood; between checkpoints, execution only. The plan assumes
the owner's real constraints: lawn job roughly 6:00-13:30 on work days, 15-30 quality
build-minutes on the morning bus, phone-only between jobs, low energy most evenings, move-in
Sept 16, grass season ending around October.

## Phase 1 - memory and truth (done 2026-08-27)

The prior chat is archived and distilled, the compass exists, Round 5 is asked. A fresh session
can resume with zero loss. **Milestone: this repo is the source of truth again.**

## Phase 2 - the demand test (to ~Sept 12)

The governing question: does a reachable, paying ICP for ALTERED exist at current audience size?

- Lock Round 5: tagline, posting account, founding tier stance, test dates and inheritor.
- Build the outreach bench in this repo (generated, disclosed, kill-switched, nothing sends on
  its own): lead sourcing runs that output candidate profiles with full reasoning; per-profile
  message drafting against locked intent and tonality; a Clack-based approve/decline review; a
  send step that is manual and per-message.
- The truth artifact: the all-in-one article (pain, promise, mechanism, buy-in), 80% and shipped.
- Fifty named ICP humans, DMed as conversations. Thresholds pre-committed: 10 real replies,
  5 problem conversations, 3 booked calls or 1 unprompted "how do I pay".

**Milestone: thresholds hit or missed, in writing.** Pass locks ALTERED through the winter
checkpoint. Miss activates the pre-named inheritor without new essays.

## Phase 3 - money rails (Sept, overlapping)

Only what a sale needs: Autumn and Resend provisioned by the owner, copy approved, page indexed,
funnel events recorded in our database. If the founding tier is confirmed, it is priced after five
real ICP conversations and closed on calls.

**Milestone: a stranger can pay us, and the first deposit or founding close.**

## Phase 4 - steady state to launch (Oct 1 to Nov 5)

Content cadence live on the approved account, outreach continuing at a sustainable daily number,
weekly funnel review from our own data. Oct 15 winter checkpoint: recurring or deposit revenue
versus the job floor, decided by the numbers on that day. Owner confirms the launch date once
sales are consistent (D024).

**Milestone: Nov 5 launch with the mechanism core, cohort onboarded, refund window opens.**

## Standing rules that protect the plan

- Between checkpoints, path challenges are logged, not acted on. A challenge must quote and defeat
  the recorded reason it overturns (direction record).
- The bet never carries the rent. The floor (job, then winter floor) is decided by calendar, not
  by optimism.
- Anything ALTERED-core is hand-written by the owner in his repo; everything here is generated and
  disclosed; outbound writes stay default-deny with per-operation approval and kill switches.
